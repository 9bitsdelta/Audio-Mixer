The summary of the diploma project of the student:
MOREANU P.D. Vlad-Ștefan, group 441G

Study program: Telecommunication systems and technologies

{\HUGE Three channel stereo audio mixer}

Scientific coordinators:
Prof. dr. ing. Monica Dascălu;
As. drd. ing. Costin-Andrei Brătan

Project goals: The purpose of the project is the practical implementation,
with discrete components of the audio mixer with output of both the mixed analog
audio and channel inputs as digital signals, meant to be transferred via USB to
a PC workstation for digital processing. Each channel will have a three band
equalisation stage (before mixing) and the analog to digital conversion will
respect the CD Audio standard specifications.
The project involves two design stages with the goal of measuring the
performance parameters of the audio mixer. The first stage consists of a
theoretical model, created in the SIMetrix simulation engine. The second stage
is the practical implementation of the mixer, requiring the design of a PCB on
which the required circuitry will be assembled. For the design, the KiCAD PCB
editor is used.

Project development and results: A simulation schematic was created in SIMetrix
v9.1 according to common mixer architecture and design principles. Given the
limitations imposed by the thesis of the project, the model was created while
taking into account the non-ideal elements of common design such as noise,
supply voltages, limited headroom and input and output impedances.
